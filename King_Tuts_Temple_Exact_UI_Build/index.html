THE ROYAL ARCHIVES
Paytable & rules
20 fixed paylines. Three or more matching symbols, left to right, starting on the first reel. The highest win on each line is paid.
THE CROWN WILD
Substitutes for all paying symbols, never for scarabs or BONUS symbols. A run of wilds pays its own prize when that is the highest award.
THE TREASURE TEMPLE
3 / 4 / 5+ emerald scarabs and BONUS symbols combined anywhere pay 2× / 5× / 20× the total bet and award 8 / 12 / 16 free spins. The azure beetle is a separate paying symbol, not a scatter.
ANUBIS AWAKENS
During free spins, every reel containing Anubis (the live symbol or the guardian tile) becomes a full wild reel for that spin. The first free spin guarantees one Anubis reel. Wild expansion replaces all three positions, including any scarabs or BONUS tiles on that reel.
YOUR FREE SPINS
3+ remaining scarab/BONUS symbols retrigger 3 free spins, up to 50 total spins in one feature. The starting level and coin value stay locked until the feature ends.
Displayed line prizes are for level 1. Multiply by the selected level. The BET display shows play credits staked: 20 × level × coin value. Coin value and bet level can be adjusted from the controls and Settings. BALANCE shows the same play-credit units. All balances and prizes are fictional.
